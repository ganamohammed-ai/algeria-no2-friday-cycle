# Data provenance and rights

The MIT licence covers the authors' code. CC BY 4.0 covers author-owned derived tables, analysis outputs and accompanying data documentation only. It does not replace the licences or terms of upstream products. Preserve the following citations and source metadata when redistributing exports.

* Sentinel-5P TROPOMI OFFL/RPRO NO2 and averaging kernels: Copernicus Sentinel data; https://sentiwiki.copernicus.eu/web/s5p-products and https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/COPERNICUS_S5P_OFFL_L3_NO2 . Processor metadata are preserved in Level-2 clips.
* ERA5: ECMWF/Copernicus Climate Change Service, Hersbach et al. (2020), https://doi.org/10.1002/qj.3803 and the applicable dataset terms at https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/ .
* EDGAR v8.1 NOx 2022: European Commission JRC, https://edgar.jrc.ec.europa.eu/dataset_ap81 . Preserve the downloaded metadata/README and attribution; mass-basis and footprint limitations in the article remain.
* ESA WorldCover: the supplied descriptor and screening scripts use v200 (2021), https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/ESA_WorldCover_v200 . The manuscript previously cited v100 (2020); see SOURCE_DISCREPANCIES.txt before reconciling the citation.
* WorldPop 2020: https://www.worldpop.org/ ; VIIRS annual night lights: Elvidge et al. (2021), https://doi.org/10.3390/rs13050922 ; SRTM: Farr et al. (2007), https://doi.org/10.1029/2005RG000183 . Static source rasters are not included; site summaries and selection candidates are included.

Original export scripts describe quality masking, regions and units. The analysis is observational and does not establish causal emissions changes. Historical plans and their deviations are retained verbatim, including terminology subsequently revised in the article. A plan-file hash is not a public preregistration timestamp.
